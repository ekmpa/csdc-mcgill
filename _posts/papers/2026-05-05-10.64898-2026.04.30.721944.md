abstract_fr: Despite growing understanding of the benefits of having Findable, Accessible,
  Interoperable, and Reusable (FAIR) data, many datasets still cannot be shared. Federated
  analysis methods can enable multisite studies that do not require the sharing of
  participant-level information. However, there are many practical hurdles that prevent
  the large-scale adoption of federated methods. We discuss challenges related to
  cross-site data preparation for federated learning, present solutions offered by
  recent neuroinformatics projects, and showcase an example of tool integration applied
  to neurodegenerative disease data.
