abstract_fr: Algebra is often challenging to teach and learn.While large 
  language models (LLMs) are gaining attention in education, their role in 
  algebra remains unclear.This systematic review, guided by the PRISMA 
  framework, analyzed 21 studies from 532 records.Findings revealed that LLMs 
  were mostly used on secondary data, for assessment, with ChatGPT, using 
  zero-shot prompts but struggled with complex reasoning.This review offers 
  theoretical insights and practical guidance for effectively applying LLMs in 
  algebra education.
