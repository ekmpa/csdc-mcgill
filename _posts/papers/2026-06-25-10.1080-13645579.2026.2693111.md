abstract_fr: 'This paper addresses the methodological challenges of surveying migrant
  populations – a hard-to-reach group due to limited sampling frames, small population
  sizes, and multi-sited data complexities. Using Meta’s platforms, Facebook and Instagram,
  we explore the potential of social-media-driven survey sampling (SMDS) for reaching
  migrants. Our contribution is threefold: first, we review strands of social-media-based
  survey research and situate migrant-focused studies within this literature. Second,
  drawing on a study of Nigerian expatriates across 11 countries, we offer six key
  practices for enhancing recruitment: assessing feasibility through reach estimates,
  establishing credibility via institutional affiliation, customizing advertisements
  to connect with subgroups, engaging a target-group moderator, maintaining communication
  and transparency, and assessing and improving representativeness. Third, we draw
  on both qualitative and quantitative evidence from our study to illustrate how these
  practices operate. Our findings suggest these practices can improve recruitment
  success in migrant-focused SMDS studies and offer practical guidance relevant to
  similar hard-to-reach populations.'
