abstract_fr: No one starts the complex process of transition with the 
  expectation of failure. However, there will be moments when the separation 
  between success and failure appears razor thin. Are there ways to anticipate 
  and avoid these potential pitfalls? What happens if things do get off track? 
  How can firms salvage the process before it's too late? One of the key areas 
  of failure lies in the founder being unwilling–or unsure of his or her 
  ability–to disconnect from the business. How can founders and successors 
  figure out what's next? What happens if the impasse can't be overcome? How do 
  founders and successors process the reality that the transition just might not
  happen?
