abstract_fr: Influential models of impression formation have found at least two major
  dimensions underlying social perception, which reflect judgments of a target's intentions
  (Warmth/Trustworthiness) and that target's ability to enact those intentions (Competence/Dominance).
  However, more recent work suggests that 'competence' and 'dominance' are not strongly
  related. What accounts for this inconsistency? This pre-registration outlines a
  study in which we explore the role of evaluative context in face perception. We
  hypothesize that impressions of ability (i.e., competence, dominance) depend on
  whether these evaluations are taking place in an 'intelligence' or 'strength' context.
