abstract_fr: This paper focuses on the role of sensitivity analysis in studies 
  of water transport in the environmental and geophysical sciences. Sensitivity 
  analysis is useful in model development to determine how changes in parameter 
  input will affect a model's response. It is also used in data assimilation to 
  incorporate observations into a model. The adjoint method allows an efficient 
  calculation of a model's sensitivity when the number of parameters exceeds the
  number of observations. In this paper we present studies demonstrating the use
  of the adjoint method for modelling water transport in both the Earth's crust 
  and mantle.
