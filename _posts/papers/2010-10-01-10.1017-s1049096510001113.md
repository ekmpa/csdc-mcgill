abstract_fr: Abstract In recent decades, the scientific forecasting of election 
  outcomes has made great strides in a number of advanced industrial 
  democracies. One country that has not received much attention to date is 
  Canada. In this article, we present a vote function model to forecast Canadian
  federal elections. We explain our model's theoretical underpinnings and assess
  its statistical properties and forecasting capabilities against all federal 
  elections held between 1953 and 2008. We then explore potential ways for 
  improving the model's accuracy. We conclude by discussing the forecast of 
  different hypothetical scenarios for an upcoming federal election.
