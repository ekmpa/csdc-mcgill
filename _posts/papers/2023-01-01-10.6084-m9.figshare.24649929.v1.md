abstract_fr: "The 2023 SiE dataset on European solidarity is built on a large yearly
  survey designed by the EUI 'Solidarity in Europe' research team and implemented
  by YouGov. This is the sixth wave of the survey, fielded between 16 March and 24
  April 2023, covering a number of topics including: European attitudes, public support
  for cross-border and domestic solidarity and redistribution, also against hypothetical
  crisis scenarios; evaluation of different national and supranational redistributive
  public policy instruments; political parties and attitudes towards democracy and
  national and international political institutions; salience of issues and threats
  facing Europeans, including attitudes on security and defence (e.g. Russia, Ukraine,
  NATO and a European army), inflation, health, housing, among others; strength of
  national and European identities and individual perceptions concerning immigration,
  the economy, political ideology, religion and voting preferences; as well as classical
  demographic indicators. The survey has inquired 24.261 respondents in 16 EU countries
  (Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Sweden, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Lithuania, Netherlands,
  Poland, Romania, Spain, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Croatia) and the United Kingdom."
