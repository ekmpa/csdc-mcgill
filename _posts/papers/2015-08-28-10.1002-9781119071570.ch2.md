abstract_fr: Merely replacing the founder is not as effective as using the 
  transition process to also remake the firm to better serve its clients. 
  Pursuing that "road less traveled" is especially difficult for the successor, 
  who must manage that operational change while also coping with the financial 
  and emotional burdens of transition. This chapter urges successors to focus on
  improvements in the firm's communications, advertising, strategies for client 
  meetings, and personnel assessment and points out the supporting role that 
  founders can and should play in that transformation. Making decisions about 
  where the firm needs "best in class" performers–and who they are–is a crucial 
  step in assigning accountability for desired results.
