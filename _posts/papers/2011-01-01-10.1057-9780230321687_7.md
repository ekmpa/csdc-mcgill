abstract_fr: The presidential election campaign constitutes one of the most 
  important moments of modern-day French political life. Each candidate uses the
  campaign as a means to present their ideas, positions, and policy proposals. 
  In addition to promoting their past achievements, candidates also seek to 
  underline the weaknesses of their adversaries. During this short period of a 
  few weeks leading up to election day, the main objective is to influence the 
  opinion of the electorate. In France as elsewhere, it is widely believed that 
  presidential campaigns matter and that the final outcome of a campaign is 
  largely attributable either to the messages conveyed during this period or to 
  the candidates’ smart moves or faux pas.
