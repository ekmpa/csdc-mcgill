abstract_fr: Successful transitions always involve achieving an acceptable 
  middle ground where everybody can win. Founders must learn to compromise some 
  objectives in order to achieve others. Prioritization, by both founders and 
  successors, is a necessary component of successful negotiation. Settling the 
  issue of appropriate ongoing compensation, commensurate with actual ongoing 
  contribution, is key. "Milking" the business to maximize current cash flows to
  founders will likely fail to maximize the firm's equity value and may doom a 
  goal to have the firm remain independent, likely forcing a sale other than to 
  internal successors, perhaps to the detriment of the firm's most important 
  constituents, its clients.
