abstract_fr: Abstract COVID-19 is a huge catastrophe of global proportions, and this
  catastrophe has had far-reaching effects on energy production worldwide. In this
  paper, we build traditional statistical models and machine learning models to forecast
  energy production series in the post-pandemic period based on Chinese energy production
  data and COVID-19 Chinese epidemic data from 2018 to 2021. The experimental results
  showed that the optimal models in this study outperformed the baseline models on
  each series, with MAPE values less than 10. Further studies found that the LightGBM,
  NNAT and LSTM machine learning models worked better in unstable energy series, while
  the ARIMA statistical model still had an advantage in stable energy time series.
  Overall, the machine learning models outperformed the traditional models during
  COVID-19 in terms of prediction. Our findings provide an important reference for
  energy research in public health emergencies, as well as a theoretical basis for
  factories to adjust their production plans and governments to adjust their energy
  decisions during COVID-19.
