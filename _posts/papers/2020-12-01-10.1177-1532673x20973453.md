abstract_fr: What has been the response of U.S. governors to the COVID-19 
  pandemic? In this research note, we explore the determinants of implementing 
  stay-at-home orders, focusing on governors’ characteristics in the early stage
  of the pandemic. In our most conservative estimate, being a Democratic 
  governor increased the probability of implementing a stay-at-home order by 
  more than 50%. Moreover, we found that the probability of implementing a 
  statewide stay-at-home order was about 40% more likely for governors without 
  term limits than governors with term limits. We also found that Democratic 
  governors and governors without term limits were significantly faster to adopt
  statewide orders than Republican governors and governors with term limits. 
  There is evidence of politics as usual in these unusual times.
