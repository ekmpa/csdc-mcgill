abstract_fr: 'In Part I we present data that describe the degree of programmatic structuration
  of Latin American party systems. Each chapter focuses on one of four indicators:
  the dimensionality of the issue space (Chapter 2), left-right semantics (Chapter
  3), elite-mass programmatic linkage on key issues or representation (Chapter 4),
  and the ideological cohesion of individual parties (Chapter 5). The data that we
  use to measure each of these indicators come from the 1997 Parliamentary Elites
  of Latin America (PELA) project and, in the case of representation, are complemented
  by data from the 1998 Latinobarometer survey. Full descriptions of the PELA survey,
  the variables we use, and a discussion of several data issues are presented in Appendixes
  A–C and in Web Appendixes D and E (at www.cambridge.org/9780521114950).'
