abstract_fr: 'The debate on privatisation, which now concerns Central and Eastern
  Europe as it transitions to a market-based economy, has centred mostly on the speed
  of the process, leaving aside important aspects related to the implementation of
  proper institutional frameworks. The work discusses this issue, reviewing the privatisation
  experiences of Central and Eastern European countries to date. The theoretical rational
  for privatisation in these countries is identified, various methods are reviewed
  and their application to the countries in question are addressed. Targets, constraints
  and implications are identified and a preliminary assessment is provided. JEL Codes:
  P20, P30'
