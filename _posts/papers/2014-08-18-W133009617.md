  that are based on aggregate data sources. We show that aggregation bias strongly
  inflates estimates of the relationship between immigrantsâ€™ educational attainment
  and the educational attainment of their children. Compared to natives, the educational
  transmission process between parent and child is much weaker in immigrant families.

  or ethnic networks, may render group characteristics more important predictors of
  second generation educational attainment than parental education.
