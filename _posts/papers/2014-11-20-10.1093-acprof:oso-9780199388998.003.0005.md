abstract_fr: Abstract This chapter shows that leaders of developing countries 
  form preferential trading agreements (PTAs) with the EU and the US when the 
  leaders have recently gained power and their countries have recently 
  democratized. The chapter offers an intuitive summary of a statistical 
  analysis, illustrates patterns in the data, and considers alternative 
  explanations. The chapter is based on a detailed statistical analysis of PTA 
  formation across the world between the years 1990 and 2007. The findings 
  provide strong and robust empirical support for the theory. The chapter also 
  shows that the factors that predict trade treaties between developing 
  countries and the EU or the US do not predict such treaties among other pairs 
  of countries, highlighting the importance of economic major powers.
