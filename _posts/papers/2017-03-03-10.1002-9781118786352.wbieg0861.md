abstract_fr: The field of electoral geography comprises the study of spatial 
  voting patterns, election campaigns and political parties, and electoral 
  systems – particularly systems that use territorial constituencies. The three 
  major approaches are the geography of voting (cartographic representation of 
  votes), geographic influences on voting, and the geography of representation 
  (the analysis of election systems). Most studies in electoral geography focus 
  on the United Kingdom and United States, which partly reflects the dominance 
  of a few extremely productive scholars.
