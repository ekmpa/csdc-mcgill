abstract_fr: This article compares two different measures of gender identity. 
  Drawing on an online survey conducted with a representative sample of 
  Americans that included both a single bipolar scale and separate masculinity 
  and femininity scales, we compare how the same people respond depending on how
  gender identity is measured. The results of validation tests suggest that a 
  single bipolar scale performs just as well in differentiating among those with
  sex-typical gender identities but separate masculinity and femininity scales 
  are needed for studies investigating the political behaviour and preferences 
  of those with sex-atypical identities.
