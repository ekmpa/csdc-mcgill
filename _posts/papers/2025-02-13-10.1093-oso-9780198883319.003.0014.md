abstract_fr: 'Abstract This chapter embeds the book in the broader literature on political
  culture and democratic attitudes. It summarizes the book’s succinct themes and gives
  an overview of the findings in tabular and descriptive form. The major contributions
  of the book discussed here are the expansion of the behavioral paradigm of political
  culture research by specifying the support for four different dimensions of democracy:
  liberal, social, direct, and populist in a controlled comparative and over-time
  design with the same concepts and indicators. It also delivers an important conceptual
  and empirical distinction between support or views, evaluation and legitimacy of
  democracy. New ways of how the data can be used to further expand the insights on
  the potential for democratic backsliding or democratic stability are outlined as
  well.'
