abstract_fr: The financial aspects of transition are usually put forward as the 
  major hurdles of a successful transition. This chapter argues that agreement 
  about the firm's financial transition can happen only after a foundation has 
  been built between the founder and successor. This foundation is formed 
  through the personal relationship of founder and successor, relying on how 
  successful both parties are in developing a shared vision for the future of 
  the firm. How should founders and successors communicate? How do they get to 
  develop rules of engagement about working styles and personality differences? 
  How are disagreements going to be settled? The successor-and-founder dynamic 
  is important as the successor tries to create a future vision supported by, 
  but not dictated by, the founder.
