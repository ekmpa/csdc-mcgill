abstract_fr: Many commentators attributed Donald Trump’s surprise 2016 election victory
  to the role played by voters heavily affected by deindustrialization, often located
  in the so-called Midwestern ‘Rust Belt’. In new research which examines voting patterns
  in counties which have experienced manufacturing layoffs, Leonardo Baccini and Stephen
  Weymouth find that that these layoffs are associated with greater support for Republican
  challengers among whites compared to voters of color. They attribute this pattern
  to whites’ concerns about social and economic status loss in the face of layoffs,
  concerns which are played on by populist and reactionary candidates like Donald
  Trump.
