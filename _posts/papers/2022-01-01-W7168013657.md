abstract_fr: This is a cross-cultural study examining clothing-driven perceptions
  of wealth in both Indian and US samples. Participants from both samples will provide
  perceptions of wealth of targets wearing both Western clothing (e.g., tuxedo, gym
  clothes) and Indian clothing (e.g., kurta, lehenga). Separate sets of participants
  will estimate the cost of the clothing worn by these targets. We will use these
  data to understand the role of cultural knowledge in clothing-based perceptions
  of wealth.
