abstract_fr: Recently, we proposed an original statistical model for forecasting
  general elections in the United Kingdom, based on the observation of a few key
  indicators of the political and economic system. That vote function model was 
  tested against the results of the 2001 general election. Here we evaluate the 
  results of that test, and offer an appropriately revised model for the 
  forecasting of the upcoming 2005 general election. According to our forecast, 
  a Labour victory appears the most likely outcome.
