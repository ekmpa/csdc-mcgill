abstract_fr: This chapter offers a survey of the literature exploring the effect
  of preferential trade agreements (PTAs) on economic reform. The main findings 
  are two-fold. First, there is evidence that north-south PTAs are associated 
  with microeconomic reform in southern countries. The main mechanism proposed 
  here is that trade agreements help leaders to tie their hands in relation to 
  reform. Second, services are important actors in promoting preferential 
  liberalization in the USA, since changes in policies and regulations in 
  developing countries help competitive US services firms to enter into these 
  liberalizing markets.
