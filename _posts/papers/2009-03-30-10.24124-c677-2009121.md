abstract_fr: After having been in power for just a little over a year and a 
  half, the Parti Québécois government led by Pauline Marois decided to call an 
  early election in the hope of getting the majority of seats that had evaded 
  them the last time around. That bet was lost as the 2014 election campaign 
  resulted in the worst showing for the PQ since 1973. The inter-election period
  was dominated by the debate over the PQ’s proposed Charter of Quebec Values. 
  The Charter also influenced the debate during the campaign, as did the issue 
  of Quebec’s sovereignty due in part to the arrival of Pierre Karl Péladeau as 
  a star candidate for the PQ. The Liberal Party of Quebec is now back in 
  government with a majority of seats and under the leadership of Philippe 
  Couillard.
