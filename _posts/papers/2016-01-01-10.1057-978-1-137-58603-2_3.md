abstract_fr: 'This chapter first briefly outlines the history and structure of immigration
  to Germany. It then explains the diversity of its cities and of city neighbourhoods.
  This includes discussion of immigrant concentration, extent and features of spatial
  segregation and nation origins of the immigrant population. Drawing on our own ‘area
  explorations’ we present key features of the 50 neighbourhoods investigated in this
  study: the structure of diversity, its visibility, the character of the public space.'
