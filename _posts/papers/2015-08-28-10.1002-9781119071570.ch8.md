abstract_fr: Beyond the operational and financial elements of succession, this 
  chapter begins the discussion of the most important aspect of all–the 
  emotional issues. The transition process is very highly emotionally charged. 
  Is an office just an office? It depends on whom you ask. Whether someone is 
  being reasonable in the process depends on the point of view of each of the 
  parties. The same process, decisions, and feelings can all be viewed 
  differently depending on which side of the table you sit on. In addition to 
  the often-cited emotional burdens for transitioning founders, successors have 
  fears, too, that they seldom admit, especially the fear of being wrong.
