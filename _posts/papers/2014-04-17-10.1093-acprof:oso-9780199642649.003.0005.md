abstract_fr: This chapter turns to the local level and examines the territorial 
  segmentation of party–voter linkages in Chile and Uruguay, based on 
  qualitative evidence from field research on seven electoral districts and 
  nineteen municipalities in Chile and seven Uruguayan municipalities, which are
  also senatorial and lower-chamber districts. The research, conducted in 
  2001–2003, reveals greater personalization and territorial segmentation in 
  Chile than Uruguay. Coupled with Chile’s low local and legislative turnover, 
  the chapter finds Chile’s local politics to be far more “individualized.”
