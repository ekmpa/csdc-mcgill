abstract_fr: Over the last ten years, Oosterhof and Todorov’s valence-dominance model
  has emerged as the most prominent account of how people evaluate faces on social
  dimensions. In this model, two dimensions (valence and dominance) underpin social
  judgments of faces. To which world regions this model applies is a critical, yet
  unanswered, question. We will address this question by replicating Oosterhof and
  Todorov’s methodology across multiple world regions.
