abstract_fr: One of the best ways to fail in the transition process is to allow 
  greed or fear to take over. Absent a true understanding of what is "enough," 
  both founders and successors can push too far in the financial negotiation. 
  The parties need to walk through this process together, helping each other 
  define how equity and cash will be shared now and into the future. They need 
  to help each other determine the ongoing rules of the game for the firm. Will 
  non–operator owners be allowed? Will there be a mandatory retirement age? 
  Should management be separated from ownership? How do firms determine the 
  major questions they need to ask, and how do they begin to answer them?
