abstract_fr: We developed a theoretical framework that encompasses a broad 
  family of misfit functions between real and simulated seismogram data, 
  including well-known examples such as the least-squares criterion. Within this
  framework, we are exploring several proximal-type algorithms to improve the 
  solution of the inverse problem. Misfit functions based on Optimal Transport 
  (OT) are formulated as specific instances of our general approach. All 
  computational experiments are implemented in Python, using the Devito package 
  for high-performance finite difference modeling.
