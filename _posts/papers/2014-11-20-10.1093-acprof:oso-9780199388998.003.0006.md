abstract_fr: 'Abstract This chapter demonstrates that EU and US preferential trading
  agreements (PTAs) are associated with major economic reforms. The chapter begins
  by explaining how major structural breaks can be detected in the data. Next, the
  chapter analyzes the timing of major economic reforms: we find that developing countries
  often implement them during negotiations and upon signature, and rarely at other
  times. The chapter also shows that countries that never negotiate a PTA rarely implement
  major economic reforms, even if one restricts attention to those countries that
  are similar to the negotiating ones. The effects of other international institutions,
  such as the IMF or the WTO, on economic reform are weaker.'
