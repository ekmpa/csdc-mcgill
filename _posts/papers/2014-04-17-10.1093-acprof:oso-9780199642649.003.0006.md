abstract_fr: Abstract This chapter moves from the systemic to the individual 
  party level, comparatively analyzing the most electorally successful parties 
  in these two countries. In Chile, the strategy of the rightist UDI is the most
  segmented and strategically harmonized, while in Uruguay, the most 
  simultaneously segmented and harmonized electoral strategy is that of the FA. 
  The fact that the UDI and FA, while competing in two different systems, have 
  both been able to successfully pursue segmented but harmonized linkage 
  strategies while other parties in each system have been much less successful 
  in doing so suggests that individual-party characteristics, not just systemic 
  or country-level traits, play a relevant role in causing the observed linkage 
  strategies.
